Adi Toledano-Shubi

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Physical Therapy at the University of Haifa, working with Prof. Einat Kodesh. I completed my PhD in the same department under the supervision of Dr. Hilla Sarig Bahat and Prof. Hagit Hel-Or.

My research develops and evaluates remote solutions for fall prevention among older adults: establishing the validity and feasibility of telehealth fall-risk assessment in the home, designing and testing online fall-prevention training programs, and using machine learning to shorten and personalize fall-risk assessment. My current work asks who is suitable for remote training, with a focus on pre-frail and frail older adults.

Alongside my research, I have taught at the University of Haifa since 2008, and I spent twelve years directing physiotherapy services at the Galilee Medical Center.
